ABOUT US

Vanprastha Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to the holistic and sustainable development of rural regions, guided by values rooted in tradition, ecological responsibility, and inclusive progress.

Our name, VANPRASTHA , is an acronym that reflects our mission:

Village Awareness towards Nature, Protection of Resources,Afforestation,Sustainability,Tradition,Harmony & Action.

KEY OBJECTIVES

Environmental Sustainability
We promote the efficient management of natural resources, support afforestation and ecological restoration, and foster region-specific sustainable development through renewable energy, organic farming, and biodiversity conservation.
Cultural & Social Empowerment
We support indigenous artisans, preserve traditional arts, and foster inclusive, peaceful communities by addressing inequality, promoting unity, and celebrating cultural identity.
Health & Well-being
We work to enhance rural health by providing access to quality healthcare, sanitation, maternal and child welfare, nutritional awareness, and preventive medical practices.
Education & Skill Development
We ensure inclusive education and lifelong learning through improved access to schools, adult literacy programs, and vocational training—empowering individuals and boosting employability.

PREVIOUS INITIATIVES

Project Punarjeevan

During the harsh summer, vast stretches of forest around Kukuchhina and Dunagiri were ravaged by wildfires, resulting in the loss of hectares of precious green cover. As the monsoon approaches, Vanprastha Foundation is committed to reviving these hills by planting thousands of native, endemic trees — bringing life and resilience back to our forests.

Empowering village children through holistic growth and quality education.

Our dedicated member and former Aeronautical Engineer at DRDO, Mr. Krishan Lal Khatter, conducted a special" Masti Ki Pathshala" for local village children. Through engaging teaching and heartfelt motivation, he inspired them to pursue their studies with enthusiasm. The children also received essential school supplies, making the special Sunday class both joyful and meaningful.

UPCOMING INITIATIVES

Medical Camp for Villagers

As part of its mission to support rural well-being, Vanprastha Foundation will be organizing a Free Medical Camp to provide essential healthcare services to villagers in remote areas on 6th of July 2025. The camp will offer basic health check-ups, medical consultations, and free distribution of medicines with the support of some of the most experienced and credentialed doctors in the country. The initiative will also focus on spreading awareness about hygiene, nutrition, and preventive care — empowering villagers with knowledge and access to better health. By bringing quality healthcare to their doorstep, Vanprastha Foundation aims to bridge the rural health divide and further its vision of inclusive and holistic community development.
