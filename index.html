Vanprastha Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to the holistic and sustainable development of rural regions, guided by values rooted in tradition, ecological responsibility, and inclusive progress.

Our name, VANPRASTHA, reflects our mission:

Village Awareness towards Nature, Protection of Resources, Afforestation, Sustainability, Tradition, Harmony & Action.
