Education Initiative

Masti Ki Pathshala

Nurturing curiosity and empowering the next generation of rural Himalayan minds.

Transforming Lives Through Learning

Education has the power to transform lives, yet for many children in rural areas, access to quality learning remains limited. Recognizing this gap, Vanprastha Foundation initiated a heartfelt effort to bring learning closer to young minds through Masti Ki Pathshala — a joyful and engaging educational experience.

Led by Wisdom: Mr. Krishan Lal Khatter

Led by our dedicated member, Mr. Krishan Lal Khatter, a former Aeronautical Engineer associated with the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA), this special session went beyond conventional teaching.

With a unique blend of knowledge, storytelling, and motivation, he created an environment where children felt encouraged to ask questions, explore ideas, and develop a genuine interest in learning. His journey from high-level engineering to rural mentorship serves as a powerful inspiration for the children of the Himalayas.

Beyond the Classroom

Held on a Sunday, the session transformed a regular day into an opportunity for growth and inspiration. The children participated with great enthusiasm and were also provided with essential school supplies, supporting their academic journey in a meaningful way.

Masti Ki Pathshala is more than just a class — it is a step toward bridging the educational divide in rural communities. By combining mentorship, resources, and encouragement, the initiative nurtures confidence, curiosity, and a lasting love for learning.
